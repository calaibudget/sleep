Sleep: privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Sleep is a personal sleep tracker for Fitbit devices. It runs entirely in your web browser. There is no Sleep server: nobody but you, on your own device, can see your data.

What the app reads

With your permission, given on Google's own sign-in page, Sleep reads your sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, breathing rate and skin temperature from the Google Health API. Access is read-only: Sleep never writes, changes or deletes anything in your Google account.

Where your data goes

  • Your data travels directly from Google to your browser and is stored only in that browser's own storage on your device.
  • Sleep has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
  • The app's code is hosted on GitHub Pages, which serves the code only. Your data and your Google sign-in are never sent to GitHub.

How Sleep uses Google user data

Sleep uses the data only to show you your own nights, wake-ups and trends. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Remove the Sleep app from your Home Screen or clear this site's data in your browser settings to delete everything Sleep stored. You can withdraw Sleep's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the app's GitHub page.
